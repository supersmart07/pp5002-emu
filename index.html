iPod Classic Emulator

Runs Apple's real iPod Software on an emulated ARM7 chip, hard disk and LCD. You supply the software; your music goes onto the emulated disk.

Open in another tab

This iPod is running in another tab. Only one tab runs it at a time, so the two cannot overwrite each other's saved iPod.

1iPod Software

Choose an iPod Software 1.x updater: the Mac download (.dmg or .dmg.bin), the Windows installer (setup.exe), or the Firmware file inside either. It is read in this browser and kept here; nothing is uploaded.

2Music

Add songs, then sync. 44.1 kHz MP3s and WAVs go on as they are; everything else (other sample rates, AAC, FLAC, OGG, AIFF) is converted to 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3. The emulated iPod decodes them itself.

    Controls

    Scroll
    Drag around the wheel, use the mouse wheel, or ↑ ↓
    Select
    Center button or Enter
    Menu
    Top of the ring or Esc. Hold it for the backlight.
    Play / Pause
    Bottom of the ring or Space
    Skip
    Left and right of the ring or ← →
    Hold
    The switch on top, or H. Locks the buttons and the wheel.
    Reset
    Hold Menu and Play together for 5 seconds (Esc + Space), or Restart iPod above. Songs and settings stay.

    This removes the iPod Software, all songs, the iPod's settings and its saved state from this browser. The page starts over as if opened for the first time.
